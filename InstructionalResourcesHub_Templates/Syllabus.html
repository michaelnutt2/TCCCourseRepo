Late Work

Late Work

Instructor Note: Insert late work policy here. Avoid zero tolerance policies. Our focus is on student success, and because mistakes, life events, or confusion can prevent any of us from timely submissions, we recommend policies in keeping with a more student-focused philosophy. For example:

  • Consider point deductions for late major assignments in favor of “no late work” policies, which typically do not serve our students well.
  • Consider allowing students to re-write major essays to improve their scores. Writing is a process, and as writers, we improve with practice. Teaching students this process and allowing them to learn from their own errors and from your feedback can be valuable. To that end, be sure to provide students with timely feedback so that they may be able to apply that feedback on future assignments.
  • Students who have plagiarized essays should be handled in accordance with TCCD policy. This policy includes a range of consequences, from a mandatory rewrite to failure for the course. Create a clear policy that outlines the types of plagiarism and how they will be handled in your class, and when doing so consider policies that include scaled responses based on the kind of plagiarism, the intent of the student, and what would benefit the student in their learning process.  For more, please see the plagiarism policy included in this document.
