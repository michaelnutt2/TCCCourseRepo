Navigating the Course

Instructor Note: If you are using any other items in the Navigation Menu, please add them and a brief description to the list below.  When finished, delete this note.

To get started, I'd like to draw your attention to the Course Menu (the set of blue links to the left of your screen). In that menu, you will notice several links:

  • Home: Takes you to the course Home Page
  • Syllabus: Shows you course information, policies, grading info, and a list of due dates
  • Announcements: Has updates, advice, reminders, and lots more to help everyone stay on track
  • Modules: The location of all course content - lessons, readings, activities, assignments, discussion boards,  and assessments
  • Grades: Your grades, links to feedback and comments on completed work, what hasn't been graded, and your overall current average
  • Microsoft Teams meetings: Shows scheduled Teams meetings for the course with links to the meetings
  • Qwickly Attendance: Attendance
  • Office 365: Access to your Microsoft account
  • Learning Commons: Tutoring, labs, library, and supplemental instruction help
  • NetTutor: Get tutoring help

The Syllabus

You will find course information about content, attendance, grading, learning objectives and more on the Syllabus page.  It is important that you read the information on the Syllabus page carefully before beginning the course. 

Course Content

The content of the course is located on the Modules page. Once you select the links in each module, you'll see the content. Start with the first module and continue moving downward from there. Each Module contains objectives, pages that resemble online lectures, videos and other resources, quizzes, and discussion boards. Your assignments and exams will also be linked in the modules. You can refer to the Syllabus to see how your grades for the assignments will be weighted.

For this course, MO will represent Module Outcome and CLO will represent Course Learning Outcome. 

What to do now

  1. Navigate to the Start Here module.
  2. Be sure to click on each item in the Start Here module.
  3. Start at the item at the top and work to the last item at the bottom of the module.
  4. Click on the item to open it.
  5. If the item has a Mark as Done option at the bottom, click the Mark as Done option.

If you have any questions, feel free to contact me through email, or using the "Inbox" menu on the far left-hand side of your screen. I am here to help and want you to do well in the course, so do not hesitate to ask any questions you might have. Stay in touch, and I hope you enjoy the course!
